About

Hi, I'm Grant

I'm a music producer and instrumentalist from Australia.

My debut album, Colour Blind, was recorded in Brisbane and released in September 2018. I write, play and produce my own records, chasing warm tones, real instruments and a bit of colour.

When I'm not in the studio I'm building things, including this website.
