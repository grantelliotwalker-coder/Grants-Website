About

Hi, I'm Grant

I'm a music producer and instrumentalist from Australia.

My debut album, Colour Blind, was recorded in Brisbane and released in 2018. Since then I've put out a run of singles, the Total Internal Reflection EP, and my latest album, Moonlight Beams (2025). I write, play and produce my own records, chasing warm tones, real instruments and a bit of colour.

When I'm not in the studio I'm building things, including this website.
